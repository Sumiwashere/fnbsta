Food Not Bombs St Andrews

Welcome to FNB St Andrews!

We are a local, autonomous mutual aid chapter, run entirely by volunteers, helping to provide free vegan meals for everyone from potential food surplus/waste! We are part of the worldwide, loosely linked network of Food Not Bombs.

What we do:

Our community meal shares run every two weeks where our volunteers help to collect, store, cook and serve meals. We offer a variety of volunteering roles based on horizontal organising, meaning you can dedicate as little or as much time to any role you'd like!

Want to get involved?

We're always looking to welcome new volunteers for a variety of roles, from meal prep to publicity. Join our Signal chat and follow our Instagram account for more details!

Are you a St Andrews-based organisation?

If you are a St Andrews-based shop, food outlet, or organisation that sometimes has surplus produce and/or vegan food, please get in touch at foodnotbombsstandrews@protonmail.com!

We would be happy to take it off your hands for our meal shares.
